# AVASARA release notes — 2026-11-19

This is an enhanced **staging candidate**, not a certification that every opportunity is live or accurate.

## Implemented
- Preserved original site and existing data; six editable Word CV templates remain available.
- Added verified/unverified deadline filters and 7/14/30/60/90-day date filters.
- Added accessibility preferences (text scaling, contrast, motion, link underlining), keyboard-accessible controls and skip link.
- Added calendar export for tracked deadlines.
- Existing programme-specific Erasmus entries and field/major search retained.
- Existing tracker, eligibility checks, academy, research listings, interview section, and worldwide opportunities retained.

## Accuracy limits
- Most scholarship deadlines and majors remain **unverified**, and no inferred subjects should be interpreted as confirmed eligibility.
- Existing `scholarship-link-audit.csv` is a review queue, not proof of URL validity.
- Erasmus Mundus programmes must be rechecked individually every intake.
- Some opportunity URLs are general provider pages; do not call them individually audited.
- Browser-only GitHub Pages cannot reliably send automated deadline alerts or continuously verify links without an external service.
- The eligibility checker is advisory, not an official eligibility determination.

## Before publishing
Review priority programme links and dates against official pages, check responsive layout and accessibility with a browser, and replace unverified items gradually with researched records.
