# AVASARA Global Discovery Release — scope and limitations

Foundation: supplied GitHub upload candidate. Existing navigation, tracker, About, toolkit and accessibility remain.

## Implemented
- Every original opportunity retained; structured discovery subjects and major topics added without inventing eligible degree majors.
- Subject browse chips with live counts, exact-major/topic search, visible topic lists and explicit unverified eligibility.
- Additional source-linked leads in Europe, Germany, Australia, Asia and Africa, plus internships and doctoral positions. These are leads, NOT a comprehensive verified global database.
- Regional Academy guides for Europe, Oceania, Asia, Africa, Americas and Middle East.
- Word CV template gallery filters and larger structural previews, retaining six downloadable editable .docx files.
- Facebook/LinkedIn/Bluesky manual post and comment-link submission by email. No automated social scraping or private dashboard.
- Existing tracker, deadline filters, calendar export, accessibility settings and application toolkit preserved.
- CSV review queue of all listings.

## NOT completed or independently verified
- All original scholarship links, exact deadlines and eligible degree majors have NOT been individually verified.
- Topic tags are not evidence that a student's prior major is eligible. Most listings remain unclassified.
- Regional links are official programme or directory leads, not individual live vacancies.
- No automatic Facebook/LinkedIn/Bluesky comment extraction or automatic publishing.
- No secure authentication or server-side eligibility engine.
- No claim of comprehensive coverage of all worldwide positions.

Before production: verify individual links and deadlines, audit programme eligibility, test on mobile and assistive technology, and update statuses regularly.
